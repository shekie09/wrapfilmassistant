Everything on set34 tools in one place

Built for the way a film actually gets made.

Every tool is a card on your dashboard. Drag the cards into the order you work in.

Script and breakdown

Write screenplays and AV scripts, or import Final Draft, Fountain, PDF and text. Tag props, wardrobe and vehicles with color coded strips.

Schedule and call sheets

Place scenes on shoot days, build the daily call sheet, and let the program find the nearest hospital for your shoot address.

Budget and payroll

Plan every line, then track what you actually spend. Keep time cards for the crew.

Cast and crew

One roster with phone numbers and emails. Text your whole team from your own Messages app, even people who do not have Wrap!.

Releases and signatures

Deal memos and release forms signed with a finger or a mouse, saved with the production.

Shots, boards and looks

Shot lists and storyboards link to each scene. Build lookbooks and keep every image and file in a media library.

QuestionsQuick answers

Before you download.

What is a member key?

The program is free to download. A member key unlocks it. The key is a one time purchase, not a subscription.

Where do I get a member key?

Member keys are not on sale yet. Check back here soon.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet. Today Wrap! runs on Mac, Windows, iPhone and iPad.

Do my cast and crew need Wrap! installed?

No. You can text them from your own Messages app and share call sheets with anyone.

Is my work safe?

Your production lives on your computer. You can back it up to a single file at any time and restore it later.
